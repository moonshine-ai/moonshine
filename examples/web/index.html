Why it is worth the download

Moonshine's streaming models were built for live speech rather than batch files, which is where the difference shows up.

Whisper has to wait for a 30-second window before it can start work. Moonshine transcribes incrementally while you are still talking, so the final transcript lands about a tenth of a second after you stop, roughly a hundred times sooner than Whisper Large v3 on the same machine, and with a lower word error rate. On this page that means you can hold a conversation with a model running on your laptop's CPU.
